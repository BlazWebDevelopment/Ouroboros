    The bite (how it reads)

    1. Trades generate a dedicated tax slice instead of only enriching insiders.
    2. That slice is programmatically aimed at other memecoins—especially the chaotic long-tail on bonding curves.
    3. Acquired inventory is routed to burn, shrinking external supply while the story gets louder.
